# Leave, Salary and Compensation Framework

## Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
excluding public holidays. Part-time employees receive leave pro-rated by
contracted hours. Unused leave may be carried over into the next year for up to
5 days only.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical
certificate is required for any absence longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Salary Review Cycle

Compensation is reviewed every 12 months. The review window opens on 1 January
and closes on 31 March. Adjustments take effect from the first payroll of April.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses above 25 units must be approved in advance by the line
manager. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.
Receipts are mandatory for every claim above 25 units.

## Salary Bands

Engineering salaries are published in four bands: Junior, Mid, Senior and
Staff. Staff band is capped at 18 percent of the engineering headcount. Band
placement is reviewed at every salary review cycle.
