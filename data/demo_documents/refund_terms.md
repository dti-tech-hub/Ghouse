# Customer Refund and Cancellation Terms

## Refund Window

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the original purchase
date. After 30 days, only a proportional refund is issued for unused service
days.

## Cancellation Notice

A cancellation must be submitted at least 14 days before the next billing date.
Cancellations submitted with less than 14 days notice are charged at 50 percent
of the monthly fee.

## Shipping and Delivery

Physical products are shipped within 3 business days of order confirmation.
Standard delivery takes between 5 and 9 business days. Express delivery takes
2 business days and costs an additional 12 units.

## Warranty Period

All hardware carries a 24 month warranty covering manufacturing defects. The
warranty does not cover accidental damage. Warranty claims must be raised
within 14 days of discovering the defect.

## Chargebacks

Chargeback disputes must be submitted to the Payment Operations team within 10
business days of the charge appearing on a statement. Disputes filed later are
rejected automatically.
