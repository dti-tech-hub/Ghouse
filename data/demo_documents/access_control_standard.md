# Access Control and Authentication Standard

## Password Requirements

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not reuse any of the last
5 passwords. Password complexity symbols are required but do not increase the
minimum length requirement.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all production access and for all
access to customer data. Employees who cannot enrol may request an exception
from the Security Operations team, which is reviewed every 30 days.

## Access Reviews

Access to production systems is reviewed every quarter. Managers must complete
access reviews within 14 days of the review request. Accounts that are not
confirmed during a review are disabled automatically.

## Offboarding

When an employee leaves, all access is revoked within 24 hours of the leaving
date. Email forwarding remains active for 30 days for handover purposes. The
offboarding checklist must be completed within 7 days.
