# Remote Work and Data Protection Policy

## Scope

This Remote Work and Data Protection Policy applies to every employee who works
outside a company office, including contractors and long-term vendors. It
covers laptops, phones, home printers and any personal device used for company
business.

## Equipment and Encryption

Every remote worker must use a company-issued laptop. Full-disk encryption must
remain enabled at all times. Personal devices may only be used for email
calendar access and must never store customer records.

## Security Reviews

A security review is required before a remote worker is granted access to
production systems. The review is performed by the Security Operations team and
is repeated whenever the worker changes country of residence.

## Incident Reporting

Any suspected data incident must be reported to the Security Operations team
within 5 minutes of discovery. Late reporting is treated as a policy violation
and is escalated to the Compliance team.

## Data Backups

Company data is backed up nightly. Backups are encrypted at rest and retained
for 35 days. A restore drill is performed every quarter to verify that backups
are recoverable.
