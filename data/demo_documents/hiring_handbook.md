# Hiring and Interview Process Handbook

## Role Definitions

An open role is defined by a signed role description that states the required
skills, the reporting line and the salary band. Roles without a signed
description may not be advertised.

## Interview Stages

The standard interview process has 4 stages: recruiter screen, technical
screen, panel interview and the values interview. Each stage has a written
scorecard that must be completed before the next stage is scheduled.

## Time to Hire

The target time from a role being approved to an offer being accepted is 35
days. Offers expire after 7 calendar days from the date they are issued.

## Offer Documentation

Every offer must reference the salary band, the reporting line and the start
date. Verbal offers are not binding and must be followed by a written offer
within 48 hours.

## Candidate Data Retention

Application data is retained for 180 days after the last stage of an interview.
After that period all candidate records are deleted automatically.
