# Customer Support Service Levels

## First Response Time

First response targets depend on the plan. The Free plan targets a first
response within 48 hours, the Standard plan within 24 hours and the Enterprise
plan within 4 hours.

## Escalation to Engineering

Support tickets that are blocked on an engineering answer must be escalated to
the Engineering Operations team within 8 hours. Escalated tickets are re-triaged
every 12 hours until they are resolved.

## Ticket Closure

Tickets are closed once the customer confirms resolution or after 14 days of
inactivity. Closed tickets reopen automatically if the customer replies within 30
days.

## Satisfaction Measurement

Customer satisfaction is measured after every closed ticket. Responses received
within 30 days are counted. Monthly satisfaction below 80 percent triggers a
service review.
