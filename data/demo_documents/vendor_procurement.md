# Vendor and Procurement Standards

## Vendor Onboarding

New vendors must complete a security questionnaire and a data processing
agreement before any contract is signed. Vendor onboarding takes on average 20
business days and is owned by the Procurement Operations team.

## Compliance Reviews

Vendors that process customer data are audited every 12 months. Vendors that do
not process customer data are audited every 24 months. A failed audit blocks
renewal until the remediation plan is approved.

## Payment Terms

Standard payment terms are net 45 from the invoice date. Early payment within 10
days earns a 2 percent discount. Invoices without a valid purchase order
number are rejected on receipt.

## Renewal Notice

Contracts must be reviewed for renewal 60 days before the end date. Contracts
that are not reviewed within 60 days of expiry auto-renew for another 12
months unless terminated in writing.

## Access Control for Vendors

Vendor accounts are provisioned with least-privilege access and must be
reviewed every 90 days. Vendor accounts that are inactive for more than 30 days
are disabled automatically.
